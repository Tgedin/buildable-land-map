Style guide

The design system the pages are built on (ADR 0007). Colours, type, space, radius and shadow are tokens in tokens.css, on Radix Colors and Open Props. Components below use the real stylesheet.

Colour roles

Data colours

Used on the map. Three category colours (orange, cyan, violet) checked for colour-blind separation; one sequential orange ramp for floors; muted classes for what the plan sets aside. Colour is never the only cue: the panel says it in words.
